Feature list

Outlook invites for every booking

  • Every one-to-one booking becomes an Outlook invite in the organizer's calendar, with the student, tutor, CTL mailbox and (optionally) the referring counselor as attendees.
  • Move and cancel update the same invite; the student can request a new time and the tutor accepts or declines.
  • Invite status (Pending, Invite sent, Failed retrying) is visible on the tutor card, the student portal, the CTL table and the inbox.
  • Booking never waits for Microsoft: the platform records the session first and a worker sends the invite with retries.

Teams meeting links

  • Online sessions get a Teams link created with the invite; a Join Teams button appears for the tutor and the student once sent.
  • CTL can switch on Teams links for in-person sessions as well.

Tutor groups (weekly cohorts)

  • A tutor creates a named weekly group for one course: weekday, time, place, seats (default 6), first and last date, with a preview of the sessions.
  • Tutors add their own referred students; students join open groups themselves from the booking dialog and see free seats.
  • One Outlook series per group; students are added to the invite as they join. Per-week attendance is recorded per student.
  • Students can skip a week or leave a group up to 24 hours before; the tutor can move or cancel one week or end the series.

CTL creates and oversees groups

  • CTL creates a group for any tutor: picks the tutor, course, time, seats, dates and optionally the first students.
  • A live availability check tests every weekly date against the tutor's published hours and existing sessions; a clash lists the dates and suggests three free times.
  • The group goes live at once as tentative: students can join, the invite is marked tentative, and the tutor can confirm, edit or reschedule it. Only CTL ends a group it created.
  • CTL can move a running group to another tutor: past weeks stay with the old tutor, remaining weeks move, referrals follow, and everyone is notified.
  • Groups tab lists every cohort across tutors with seats, next session, invite status, Add student, Change tutor, Reschedule and End series.

CTL integration panel

  • Choose whose calendar organizes invites: the tutor's own calendar or a shared CTL mailbox; switch at any time.
  • Toggle counselor invites and Teams links for in-person sessions; test the Microsoft connection; see sync health and retry anything that failed.

What does not change

  • Login stays username and password; nobody signs in to Microsoft inside the platform.
  • Microsoft is reached by the server only, inside a mailbox policy IT controls. Tutors still see only their own referred students.
